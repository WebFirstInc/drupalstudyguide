
# Attributions

We recognize that it takes in talented people to build things. Here we like to thank those that have made contributions to this project. But first...

## Thank You, David Stinemetze - AKA "WidgetsBurritos"

We want to take a moment to honor and thank David Stinemetze, fondly known in the Drupal community as "WidgetsBurritos," for his incredible contributions, particularly his work on the "[Drupal 8 Certification Study Guide](https://github.com/WidgetsBurritos/d8-studyguide)." This resource has become a cornerstone for many, helping countless Drupal enthusiasts and professionals prepare for certifications and deepen their expertise.

As David moves on to explore new opportunities and ventures beyond the Drupal community, we reflect on the profound legacy he leaves behind. His dedication to education, community support, and excellence in Drupal has left an indelible mark on those who benefited from his work.

While his path may now lead him in new directions, his impact will continue to inspire and empower Drupal developers for years to come. David, your contributions have made a difference far greater than words can express, and your legacy will always remain a cherished part of this community.

We wish you all the best in your future endeavors, David, and thank you for everything you've given to the Drupal community!

A quick word from a correspondence with David himself:

> When I first built the study guide it was a tool for me to study with and my decision to publish it came from a place that believes in open source and collaborative work. And from that perspective it was never really mine, just my way of giving back to Drupal.
> 
> I know a lot of people got value out of it I’m glad you saw it useful to keep it alive. So thank you for breathing it some new life. You absolutely have my blessing to move forward in whatever way you see fit.
